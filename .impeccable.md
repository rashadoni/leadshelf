## Design Context

### Users

Zeytunpharm medical representatives and field managers use LeadDrive Field on
phones and Android tablets during route planning, travel, doctor/pharmacy
visits, GPS-verified workdays, task execution, and offline synchronization.
The interface must be readable in daylight and operable by touch while moving
between short operational tasks.

### Brand Personality

Calm, clinically precise, operational. The product should feel trustworthy and
purpose-built for medical field work, not decorative or consumer-like.

### Aesthetic Direction

Daylight-first, calm operational mobile UI: blue-tinted light surfaces, one
clear action blue, and explicit status colours only for evidence. Deep navy is
reserved for camera/proof surfaces, never a large dashboard hero or a second
competing product theme. The interface should be as immediately learnable as a
good phone game without becoming childish: the selected day opens that day,
the next action is obvious, and secondary tools expand from named groups. AZ
and RU are first-class interface languages.

### Design Principles

- Preserve all critical field actions across phone, tablet portrait, and tablet landscape.
- Use 44 dp minimum touch targets and avoid hover-only or precision interactions.
- On tablets, prefer a persistent navigation rail, bounded reading widths, and two-column master/detail-ready workspaces.
- Keep offline, synchronization, GPS, validation, and stale-data states explicit.
- Favor information hierarchy and field speed over decorative density.
- Do not make a user scroll past metrics or repeated cards to reach the next
  work action; use compact disclosure and a safe thumb-zone action when needed.
